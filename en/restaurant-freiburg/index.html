Restaurant in Freiburg

Restaurant in Freiburg city centre: Turkish grill cuisine at the ocakbaşı

Divan Ocakbaşı is at Bertoldstraße 54, right in Freiburg’s city centre and only about a 4-minute walk from the main station. On the open charcoal grill – the ocak – we cook köfte, Adana and lamb chops, served with meze to share, fresh pide, bakes from the stone oven and, to finish, a künefe. We are open every day from 11:30 am to 11:30 pm.

What to expect at Divan

“Ocakbaşı” literally means “sitting by the fire”. In Anatolia it is where people gather – around the grill where the meat cooks over the embers. We bring exactly this tradition to Bertoldstraße: real embers, rich spices and heartfelt hospitality.

Our menu has more than 200 dishes and drinks – from starters and meze to grill specialities, döner, pide and lahmacun, the saç pan, börek and poğaça, desserts, rakı, wine and beer. The full menu is digital in 15 languages, at the table via QR code or online.

Popular dishes

  • Karışık Et Tabağı – the mixed grill: lamb skewer, lamb chops, döner meat, meatballs and turkey skewer with rice, garlic yoghurt sauce and salad. Also available as a large platter for two.
  • Peynirli Sarımsaklı Köfte – meatballs filled with sheep’s cheese and garlic, served with rice.
  • İskender Spezial – döner on fresh sesame bread with tomatoes, garlic yoghurt and tomato sauce.
  • Karnıyarık – oven-baked aubergines with a spiced minced-meat filling, tomato sauce and butter rice.
  • Sebzeli Kiremit – a vegetable bake from the stone oven.
  • Künefe – shredded pastry with melted cheese in syrup, topped with pistachios.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Divan Ocakbaşı in Freiburg?

At Bertoldstraße 54, 79098 Freiburg im Breisgau, in the city centre. From the main station it is about 270 m, roughly a 4-minute walk.

What does ocakbaşı mean?

The Turkish word means “sitting by the fire”. An ocakbaşı is a grill restaurant where the food is prepared on an open grill.

When is the restaurant open?

Every day from 11:30 am to 11:30 pm. The lunch menu is served from 11:30 am to 4 pm. Hours may differ on public holidays.

Can I book a table?

Yes, by phone on 0761 2927151.

Is the meat halal?

Yes, the meat we serve is halal.
