Frequently asked questions

Where is Divan Ocakbaşı?

Bertoldstraße 54, 79098 Freiburg im Breisgau, in the city centre – about 270 m (roughly a 4-minute walk) from the main station. More: Restaurant near Freiburg train station.

What are the opening hours?

Every day from 11:30 am to 11:30 pm. Hours may differ on public holidays.

How can I book a table?

By phone on 0761 2927151.

Is there a lunch menu?

Yes: every day from 11:30 am to 4 pm, twenty lunch dishes at €10.90 each. More: Lunch in Freiburg.

What does ocakbaşı mean?

“Sitting by the fire”: a grill restaurant where food is prepared on an open grill. At our place that is a charcoal grill.

Is the meat halal?

Yes, the meat we serve is halal.

What dishes are there?

More than 200 dishes and drinks: starters and meze, grill specialities, oriental specialities, döner kebab, bakes from the stone oven, the saç pan, pide and lahmacun, börek and poğaça, desserts, plus rakı, wine and beer. The guide Turkish food explained explains the terms.

Is the menu available in other languages?

Yes, the digital menu is available in 15 languages: Arabic, Chinese, English, French, German, Greek, Hindi, Italian, Korean, Kurdish, Persian, Russian, Spanish, Turkish and Urdu. At the table, open it via QR code.

What vegetarian dishes are there?

These include, for example, the vegetable bake Sebzeli Kiremit from the stone oven and various meze. Please check with us before ordering whether a dish is vegetarian: 0761 2927151.

Can I have food delivered?

Yes, via Lieferando and Wolt. The delivery area and available dishes are shown in the respective app.

Are there ayran, rakı, wine and beer?

Yes: ayran is homemade, and there are also rakı, wine and beer.

What can I order for dessert?

For example künefe: shredded pastry with melted cheese in syrup, topped with pistachios.

Where can I ask about ingredients and allergens?

Please call us or ask on site: 0761 2927151. We cannot give binding information about ingredients here.
