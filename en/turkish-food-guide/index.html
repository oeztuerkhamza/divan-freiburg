Turkish food explained: what are Adana, İskender and künefe?

Turkish menus are full of terms not everyone knows. Here we explain the most important dishes – and tell you what you can find of them at Divan Ocakbaşı in Freiburg.

Grill and meat dishes

Ocakbaşı

“Ocak” is the hearth, “baş” the place in front of it: an ocakbaşı is a grill restaurant where food is cooked on an open grill – and guests often sit right at the fire.

At Divan: an open charcoal grill.

Adana şiş

Spiced, often hot minced meat (traditionally lamb) grilled over charcoal on a wide skewer. Named after the city of Adana in southern Turkey.

At Divan: Adana şiş from the charcoal grill.

Şiş

The Turkish word for skewer: pieces of meat (lamb, chicken, turkey) are threaded on a skewer and grilled.

At Divan: lamb skewer and turkey skewer in the Karışık Et Tabağı.

Köfte

Spiced meatballs or patties, grilled or fried.

At Divan: Peynirli Sarımsaklı Köfte (filled with sheep’s cheese and garlic) and Pilavlı Köfte (with rice).

İskender

Döner meat on pieces of bread, served with tomato sauce and yoghurt – traditionally with melted butter. The dish comes from Bursa.

At Divan: İskender Spezial – döner on fresh sesame bread with tomatoes, garlic yoghurt and tomato sauce.

Karnıyarık

“Split belly”: an aubergine is cut open lengthways and filled with minced meat, onions and tomatoes.

At Divan: oven-baked aubergines with a spiced minced-meat filling, tomato sauce and butter rice.

Ali Nazik

Aubergine purée with garlic yoghurt topped with fried meat – a classic from south-eastern Anatolia.

At Divan: on the lunch menu.

Saç

A saç is a domed iron plate or pan. Saç dishes are prepared on it and served hot.

At Divan: saç pan on the menu, saç döner on the lunch menu.

Starters, oven dishes and pastries

Meze

Small starters shared in the middle of the table – cold or warm, often enjoyed with rakı.

At Divan: Karışık Meze, small or large for two.

Kiremit

The Turkish word for roof tile; kiremit dishes are bakes from the stone oven.

At Divan: Sebzeli Kiremit – vegetable bake with potatoes, aubergines, courgettes, cauliflower, peppers, tomatoes and garlic.

Pide

An oblong, boat-shaped flatbread, topped or filled and baked in the stone oven.

At Divan: pide, for example Peynirli Pide with cheese.

Lahmacun

A thin dough base with a spiced minced-meat topping, often called “Turkish pizza”. Often rolled up with salad and lemon.

At Divan: Pide & lahmacun on the menu.

Börek & poğaça

Börek is filled pastry made from thin dough (for example with cheese, spinach or meat). Poğaça is a soft savoury bun.

At Divan: freshly baked börek and poğaça.

Dessert and drinks

Künefe

Fine shredded pastry (kadayıf) with melted cheese, soaked in syrup and sprinkled with pistachios – best served warm. The dessert comes from the south of Turkey.

At Divan: künefe with pistachios.

Ayran

A refreshing drink made of yoghurt, water and salt – a classic companion to grilled food.

At Divan: homemade ayran.

Rakı

An aniseed spirit usually diluted with water, which turns it milky white. Traditionally drunk with meze.

At Divan: rakı, wine and beer on the drinks menu.

Tips for your first visit

  • Want to try several specialities? The Karışık Et Tabağı combines lamb skewer, lamb chops, döner meat, köfte and turkey skewer – also available as a large platter for two.
  • Order meze to share first, so everyone gets a taste.
  • To finish, a künefe – or a glass of rakı.

Frequently asked questions

Is Turkish food spicy?

It depends on the dish. Adana is traditionally hot and spicy, many other dishes are mildly seasoned. If you have questions about spice levels, please ask us.

Which dish is good for a first visit?

The Karışık Et Tabağı (mixed grill platter) offers a cross-section, and meze to share go well with it.

What does karnıyarık mean?

“Split belly”: an aubergine filled with minced meat.

What is the difference between pide and lahmacun?

Pide is a thicker, topped flatbread in boat shape. Lahmacun is a thin dough base with spiced minced meat.
