Near the main station

Restaurant near Freiburg train station: just a 4-minute walk

Arriving in or leaving Freiburg and want to eat first? Divan Ocakbaşı is at Bertoldstraße 54, only about 270 m from the main station – roughly a 4-minute walk towards the city centre. We are open every day from 11:30 am to 11:30 pm.

Call: 0761 2927151 View the menu Get directions

From the main station to Divan

The distance from Freiburg main station is about 270 m as the crow flies, around 4 minutes on foot. For the exact route see Google Maps: walking from the main station to Divan.

Eating before you leave or after you arrive

Lunch before the next leg of your journey or dinner after arriving: we are open every day from 11:30 am to 11:30 pm, and the lunch menu runs from 11:30 am to 4 pm at €10.90 per dish (see Lunch in Freiburg). The menu is digital in 15 languages – handy for travellers from abroad.
